Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Answer Engine Optimization methodology, Reddit brand safety, and FTC compliance.

(1) How do you guarantee our brand won't get banned on Reddit?
We strictly reject automated bots, spam link posting, and newly created sockpuppet accounts. Instead, we deploy established, aged Reddit persona accounts with years of karma history across relevant technical communities. Every engagement follows our strict 90/10 Rule: 90% objective technical value, unbranded context, and genuine community participation, with at most 10% commercial relevance. We never post raw affiliate links or blatant advertisements, guaranteeing 100% brand safety and zero account bans.
(2) How does AI Search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) pick up Reddit citations?
Both OpenAI and Google maintain official real-time data API licensing partnerships with Reddit. When buyers ask commercial questions (such as "What is the best enterprise incident management tool?"), LLMs query live retrieval engines that prioritize high-sentiment Reddit threads over marketing blogs. By engineering authentic community consensus around specific high-intent comparison queries, we position your product as the dominant citation in AI-generated answers.
(3) What is the typical timeline to see pipeline impact?
Direct referral traffic and qualified demo inquiries typically begin within 2 to 3 weeks of initial seeding. Full Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) citation indexing in Perplexity and Google AI Overviews compounds over 30 to 90 days as threads gain upvotes, organic comments, and high page-1 search authority on Google.
(4) Are your practices FTC compliant?
Yes, 100%. We operate in full compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising. We do not fabricate fake reviews or make unsubstantiated claims. Our work focuses on orchestrating genuine user-experience discussions, answering real consumer questions with factual product advantages, and adhering to strict legal transparency.
(5) How do you collaborate with our in-house marketing / SEO team?
We function as a specialized extension of your in-house growth engine. Your team defines the messaging guidelines, brand boundaries, and no-go claims; we translate those requirements into community-acceptable vernacular and execute all thread seeding, dark social tracking, and AEO indexing. We provide monthly citation analytics and collaborate directly via Slack or monthly strategy calls.
